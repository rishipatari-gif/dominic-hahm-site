About

From Seoul to Sather Gate

I grew up in Seoul and came to Berkeley to study two things that keep pointing at each other: how markets price the future, and how human beings actually reason under uncertainty. Economics gave me the models. Cognitive science keeps reminding me where the models break.

That pairing shapes how I work. At Seahorn Capital Group it means stress-testing a cash-flow model until the assumptions stop hiding. At Boston Consulting Group in Seoul it meant sizing what an AI transformation is really worth to a $58B financial group. At skedAI it meant sitting through forty customer interviews before writing a single line of positioning.

Outside of that: cello, vocal jazz, nationally ranked mixed doubles badminton, a long-running argument about the SF Giants bullpen, and a PADI Advanced Open Water card I use more than I should admit.
